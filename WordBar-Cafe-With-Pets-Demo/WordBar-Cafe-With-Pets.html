Café with pets

A modern coffee bar with a glass front. Customers bring their pets, and Mei's own corgi naps in its bed by the counter. Answer on the phone to serve the customer at the counter.

How customers and pets move

  1. In: through the sliding glass door at the right, with their pet trotting behind.
  2. Order: at the counter. The pet jumps up onto the counter and waits there, wagging its tail and twitching its ears.
  3. Served: a right answer makes the pet dance; a wrong one makes its ears droop.
  4. Leave: the customer picks up the drink and walks off to the left, behind Mei. The pet jumps down and follows. The counter stops short at both ends, so you see them walk in and out in full.
